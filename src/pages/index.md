## Hello there

I’m Saad, a Senior Web UI Engineer from London, and this is my digital space.

With a strong foundation in design, I craft web experiences with a keen focus on accessibility and performance. Lately, I’ve been diving into the world of static site generators like [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/), the art of [design systems](https://www.interaction-design.org/literature/topics/design-systems), the power of [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/), and the flexibility of [micro-frontends](https://frontendmastery.com/posts/understanding-micro-frontends/).

Learn more [about me](/about/), explore my thoughtfully penned [blog](/blog/), or perhaps, extend your generosity with a [virtual cup of :coffee:](https://ko-fi.com/saadbess).
